## 4C. BLOG CONTENT BRIEF TEMPLATE

**Give this exact document to yourself or your writer before typing a single word of a blog post:**

- **Target Keyword:** [e.g., "how to hide TV wires without cutting wall uk"]
- **Secondary Keywords:** [cable tidy solutions, hide cables living room, tv wire organiser]
- **Search Intent Analysis:** Information gathering. The user is frustrated with a messy living room but doesn't want to do DIY. Offer them stylish, non-drill products.
- **Target Audience:** UK homeowners and renters, aged 25-50, who value interior aesthetics.
- **Article Goal:** Get reader to click and purchase the "Silicone Cable Organiser" and "Woven Wire Box".
- **H2 Structure:**
  - H2: Why Messy Wires Ruin Your Aesthetic
  - H2: Solution 1: The Baseboard Route
  - H2: Solution 2: Minimalist Desk Tidies (Pitch Product 1 here)
  - H2: Solution 3: Decorative Storage Boxes
- **Internal Links to Include:** Link to the Tech Gadgets category page, link to 2 specific products.
- **External Links:** Link to an article on "Electrical Safety UK" to build trust.
- **Competitors to Beat:** Google the keyword. Look at the top 3 results. Note what they miss (e.g., "They don't provide renter-friendly options. Make sure our post focuses on non-drilling solutions.")
- **Word Count Target:** 1,000 words.

## 4D. INTERNAL LINKING RULEBOOK

**Anchor Text Ratios (Crucial so Google doesnt penalise you):**
- 40% Exact Match: "...buy our **silicone cable organiser** today..."
- 30% Partial Match: "...check out our new **desk tidying accessories**..."
- 20% Branded: "...exclusively available at **Luxe Doow**..."
- 10% Generic: "...to learn more about shipping, **click here**."

**Maximum Links per Page:**
Never put more than 50 links in the body text. For a standard blog post, 3 to 5 internal product links is the sweet spot.

**Silo Structure Diagram:**
Homepage 
 ├──> Category: Tech Gadgets (pass authority down)
 │     ├──> Sub-category (optional): Cable Management
 │     └──> Product: Silicone Organiser (pass authority up to Category)
 │
 └──> Blog Home
       └──> Post: "Desk Setup Ideas" (Links horizontally to Product: Silicone Organiser)

**Old to New Rule:**
Whenever you publish a NEW blog post or product, set a timer for 10 minutes. Go to Google, type `site:yourdomain.co.uk [topic of new post]`. Find 3 old pages on your site that mention the topic. Edit those old pages to add an internal link pointing to your NEW page. This instantly gets the new page indexed.

**Orphan Page Prevention:**
If a product has zero internal links pointing to it, Google cannot find it. EVERY product must belong to a Category. EVERY standalone landing page must be linked in the footer.
