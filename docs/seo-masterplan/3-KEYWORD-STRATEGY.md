# 3. KEYWORD STRATEGY SYSTEM

## 3A. THE GOOGLE.CO.UK AUTOCOMPLETE METHOD

1. **Incognito Mode:** Open a private browsing window to stop your personal history altering the results. Navigate to `www.google.co.uk`.
2. **Seed Keyword:** Type your broad product type, e.g., "desk organiser". Do NOT hit enter.
3. **Record Suggestions:** Look at the drop-down. You will see things like "desk organiser wooden", "desk organiser argos", "desk organiser cheap". Ignore competitor names (Argos), write down the descriptive ones.
4. **Alphabet Soup:** Type "desk organiser a", write down the results. Delete the 'a', type "b". Repeat through the entire alphabet.
5. **People Also Ask:** Now hit enter on "desk organiser uk". Scroll down to the "People also ask" box. Click the arrows to expand them—more will appear. These are your FAQ and Blog Post titles.
6. **Related Searches:** Scroll to the very bottom of page 1. Look at "Related searches". These are excellent sub-category ideas.
7. **Organise:** Put all these phrases into the Keyword Spreadsheet.
8. **Prioritise Buyer Intent:** If the phrase has "buy", "price", "uk", "for sale", flag it as Priority 1 (Product page). If it has "how to", "best", "ideas", flag it as Priority 2 (Blog).
