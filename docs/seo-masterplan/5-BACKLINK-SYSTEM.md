# 5. BACKLINK ACQUISITION SYSTEM

## 5A. 15 BACKLINK TYPES EXPLAINED

1. **UK Business Directories:** (Easy, Fast, Low Value). Core foundation. Just submitting your name/address/URL to trusted UK indexes.
2. **Niche Directories:** (Medium, Fast, Medium Value). Directories specifically for "UK Interior Design" or "UK Fashion Brands".
3. **Local Citations:** (Easy, Fast, Low Value). Yelp UK, Google Business Profile (even if virtual address).
4. **HARO / Connectively:** (Hard, Slow, Very High Value). Answering journalist questions. Generates links from massive national papers (e.g., The Guardian, TechRadar).
5. **Guest Posting:** (Medium, Slow, High Value). Writing an article for another UK blog.
6. **Broken Link Building:** (Hard, Slow, High Value). Finding dead links on decor blogs and offering your product as the replacement.
7. **Resource Page Links:** (Medium, Medium, High). Reaching out to pages titled "Favourite Home Decor Links" and asking to be added.
8. **Competitor Stealing:** (Medium, Slow, High). Downloading exactly who links to your competitors and emailing those sites.
9. **Forum / Profile Links:** (Easy, Fast, Zero-Low Value). Real contributions to UK forums (e.g., Mumsnet, AVForums). Do not spam.
10. **Product Roundups:** (Medium, Medium, High). Emailing authors of "Top 10 Vases" asking to be considered for next year's list.
11. **Gift Guides:** (Medium, Seasonal, High). Reaching out to magazines in October for their "Christmas Gifts for Him" guides.
12. **Blogger Reviews:** (Medium, Medium, Medium Value). Sending a free product (you pay the AliExpress cost) to a UK blogger in exchange for a review and link.
13. **Press Releases:** (Medium, Fast, Low Value). Using a UK wire service to announce your launch.
14. **Podcast Appearances:** (Medium, Slow, High Value). Being a guest on e-commerce or interior design podcasts. Link is in the show notes.
15. **Infographic Outreach:** (Hard, Slow, High Value). Creating a beautiful graphic of "UK Home Decor Trends" and offering it to bloggers to embed with a link to you.

## 5D. COMPETITOR BACKLINK ANALYSIS (FREE METHOD)

1. **Identify Competitors:** Google your primary product ("buy neon sign uk"). Ignore Amazon/Etsy. Find an independent UK store ranking on Page 1 or 2. Copy their URL.
2. **Access Free Tool:** Go to Ahrefs Free Backlink Checker (`ahrefs.com/backlink-checker`). 
3. **Run Query:** Paste the competitor's exact product page URL (not their homepage). Click "Check backlinks".
4. **Analyse:** You will see the top 100 websites linking to that page. Look at the "Anchor Text" and the "Referring Page". 
5. **Qualify:** If a backlink comes from a blog post titled "10 Best Neon Signs", that is a round-up post. If it comes from a directory, it's a citation. 
6. **Execute:** Click through to that referring page. Find the author's contact email. Use Email Template #2 or #3 above to pitch your product as a superior or alternative option for their page.
