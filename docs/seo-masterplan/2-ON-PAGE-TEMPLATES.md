# 2. ON-PAGE SEO MASTER TEMPLATES (DAILY USE)

## 2A. PRODUCT PAGE SEO TEMPLATE

### Title Tag Template
**Formula:** `[Primary Keyword] | [Secondary Keyword] | [Brand Name]`
- **Rule:** Maximum 60 characters to avoid truncation in Google.

**Examples:**
*Custom Neon Sign:*
- **Good:** Custom Neon Name Sign UK | Bedroom Wall Light | Brand Name
- **Bad:** Neon Sign Led Light Customised Cheap Drop Shipping UK

*Desk Organiser:*
- **Good:** Cable Organiser Desk Tidy | Silicone Wire Clip | Brand Name
- **Bad:** Buy 5-Wire Silicone Desktop Organiser Tidy Desk Clean

*Kitchen Gadget:*
- **Good:** Avocado Slicer Tool | 3-in-1 Kitchen Gadget | Brand Name
- **Bad:** 3 In 1 Avocado Slicer Corer Cutter Peeler Pit Remover

### Meta Description Template
**Formula:** `[Hook + Primary Keyword + Benefit + Call to Action]`
- **Rule:** Maximum 155 characters. Must include "UK" and a delivery note.

**Examples:**
- **Good (Neon):** Design your ultimate custom neon name sign in the UK. Add a luminous, personalised glow to any bedroom. Free 10-20 day delivery. Order your sign today.
- **Good (Organiser):** Clear your workspace with our premium cable organiser desk tidy. Holds 5 wires securely. Enjoy free UK shipping on all home office tools. Shop now.
- **Good (Kitchen):** Prepare brunch perfectly with the 3-in-1 avocado slicer tool. Cut, pit, and scoop effortlessly. Ships globally direct to the UK. View our kitchen range.

### H1 Tag Rule
- **Rule:** Exact primary keyword, unique from the Title tag. Max 70 chars.
- **Examples:** 
  - Personalised Custom Neon Name Sign
  - 5-Wire Silicone Desk Cable Organiser
  - 3-in-1 Avocado Slicer and Pitter

### H2 Structure for Product Pages
1. `<H2>` Overview: The Personalised Neon Sign `</H2>`
2. `<H2>` Key Features & Lighting Details `</H2>`
3. `<H2>` Technical Specifications & Dimensions `</H2>`
4. `<H2>` UK Delivery & Returns Information `</H2>`
5. `<H2>` Frequently Asked Questions `</H2>`

### Product Description Formula
Never use the default AliExpress text. Use this 5-paragraph flow:

**Paragraph 1: The Emotional Hook**
(Sentence starter: *Imagine walking into... / Are you tired of...*)
"Imagine stepping into a bedroom that perfectly captures your personality. Our Custom Neon Name Sign guarantees you'll never have a dull, uninspired wall again."

**Paragraph 2: What it is exactly**
(Sentence starter: *Crafted from... / This premium...*)
"Crafted from energy-efficient, shatterproof silicone LED tubing, this bespoke light art mounts effortlessly to any wall. Your package includes the sign, pre-drilled acrylic backing, and a standard UK power adapter."

**Paragraph 3: Key benefits (Use British English)**
(Sentence starter: *You'll love how...*)
"You'll love how the adjustable dimmer lets you transition from a vibrant party glow to a soft, ambient nightlight. The cool-touch technology ensures it is completely safe for children's nurseries or busy home bars."

**Paragraph 4: How/Where to use it**
(Sentence starter: *Perfect for... / Whether you are...*)
"Perfect for elevating your gaming setup, styling an unforgettable wedding reception, or gifting a personalised treasure to a loved one."

**Paragraph 5: The Trust/Delivery Close**
(Sentence starter: *With our tracked... / We take pride in...*)
"We take pride in our global sourcing. Order today and enjoy secure, tracked delivery direct to your UK address within 10 to 20 working days, backed by our 14-day hassle-free returns policy."

### Image Alt Text Rules
**Formula:** `[Descriptive phrase] + [Product type] + [for] + [use case] + [UK]`
- **Good 1:** Pink cursive custom neon name sign for bedroom wall UK
- **Good 2:** Black 5-wire cable organiser desk tidy for home office UK
- **Good 3:** Green 3-in-1 avocado slicer tool for modern kitchens UK
- **Good 4:** Glowing warm white personalised neon sign for wedding reception UK

### Internal Linking Rules on Product Page
- **To Parent Category:** Link naturally in the text. *Example:* "View our full range of **custom lighting** to complete the look." (Anchor: custom lighting).
- **To Cross-sells:** *Example:* "Pairs perfectly with our **LED cloud wall light**." (Anchor: LED cloud wall light).
- **To Blog:** *Example:* "Not sure what colour to pick? Read our guide on **bedroom lighting ideas**." (Anchor: bedroom lighting ideas).

## 2C. BLOG POST SEO TEMPLATE

### Formulae
- **Title Tag:** `[Number] Best [Keyword] Ideas for a UK [Room/Setting] in [Year]`
  - *Example:* 7 Best Neon Sign Ideas for a UK Bedroom in 2025
- **Meta Description:** `Struggling for [Topic] inspiration? Discover the [Keyword] to transform your space. Read our complete guide for UK homes and shop the looks today.`
- **H1:** How to Style Neon Signs in a UK Bedroom (2025 Guide)

### Article Structure
1. **Introduction:** Hook the reader. State the problem (boring bedroom walls). Introduce the solution (neon signs).
2. **H2: Why LED Neon is Replacing Traditional Glass in 2025**
   - *H3: Energy Efficiency during UK Winters*
   - *H3: Child and Pet Safety*
3. **H2: Top 3 Ways to Position Your Sign**
   - *H3: Above the Headboard* (Include image + alt text: `Pink custom neon name sign above double bed UK`)
   - *H3: The Vanity Mirror Setup*
4. **H2: Colour Psychology: Choosing the Right Glow**
5. **Conclusion:** Summarise. Clear Call to Action -> "Ready to create yours? **Design your custom neon sign here**."

### Linking requirements
- **Internal:** Minimum 3 links to exact products mentioned, 1 link to the main category.
- **External:** 1 link to an authoritative, non-competing UK source (e.g., an Ideal Home magazine trend report or BBC article on energy efficiency).

   - **CTA Button:** Shop New Arrivals.

5. **Featured Categories (H2):** Grid linking to Home Decor, Tech, Kitchen.
6. **Bestsellers (H2):** Carousel of top 8 products.
7. **UK Trust Section (H2):** "Trusted by UK Customers". Real reviews from UK buyers mentioning the quality made the 15-day wait worth it.
8. **Latest Journal (H2):** SEO blog loop.
9. **Footer:** Links to FAQ, Delivery, Returns, Contact, Terms, Schema data location.
