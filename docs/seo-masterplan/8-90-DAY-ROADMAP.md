# 8. 90-DAY IMPLEMENTATION ROADMAP

## DO THIS TODAY (IMMEDIATE ACTIONS)
1. **Pass File 1 to Developer:** Send `robots.txt`, `sitemap.xml` instructions, JSON-LD schemas, and Canonical rules to your dev to implement into the Next.js codebase.
2. **Claim Search Console:** Verify your domain ownership in Google Search Console and submit the sitemap once live.
3. **Audit Meta Titles:** Go to your top 3 selling products right now and rewrite their Title Tags using the strict 60-character formula.
4. **Update the Delivery Page:** Rewrite your shipping policy page using the "asset narrative" (File 7E) to build trust.
5. **Set up Google Analytics 4:** Ensure GA4 is tracking organic traffic correctly.

## MONTH 1: TECHNICAL & STRUCTURAL FOUNDATION
*Theme: Laying the concrete slab so Google can index your platform safely.*

- **Week 1: Core Tech Check**
  - Execute Developer Handoff (Schemas, Sitemaps, Core Web Vitals check).
  - Review GSC daily for initial crawl errors.
  - *Time required: 4 hours + Dev time.*
- **Week 2: Keyword Mapping & Homepage**
  - Run the Google.co.uk Autocomplete method (File 3A).
  - Populate the Spreadsheet with your exact 50 targets.
  - Rewrite your Homepage Meta Title, Meta Description, and H1.
  - *Time required: 5 hours.*
- **Week 3: Purging Dropship Data (Batch 1)**
  - Select your Top 10 Highest Margin / Highest Priority products.
  - Delete AliExpress content. Apply the 5-Paragraph Formula and Alt Text rules.
  - *Time required: 8 hours.*
- **Week 4: Purging Dropship Data (Batch 2)**
  - Continue rewriting the next Top 20 products.
  - Rewrite all Category Page descriptions (150 words per category).
  - *Time required: 8 hours.*

---

## MONTH 2: CONTENT & ON-PAGE
*Theme: Feeding the algorithm unique, helpful UK-focused content.*

- **Week 5: The Blog Launch**
  - Setup `/blog` section on the site.
  - Publish Blog Post #1 (Informational intent from your keyword sheet).
  - Link from the blog post to 3 products.
  - *Time required: 6 hours.*
- **Week 6: Weekly Rhythm & Internal Linking**
  - Publish Blog Post #2.
  - Check the "Old to New Rule" (File 4D) and link old pages to the new blog.
  - *Time required: 4 hours.*
- **Week 7: Diagnostic Check**
  - Open GSC. Review the "Pages" report. Look for "Discovered - not indexed".
  - If found, revisit those product pages, add more unique wording, click "Request Indexing".
  - Publish Blog Post #3.
  - *Time required: 5 hours.*
- **Week 8: Expanding the Hubs**
  - Publish Blog Post #4.
  - Finish rewriting any remaining legacy AliExpress product descriptions.
  - *Time required: 6 hours.*

---

## MONTH 3: AUTHORITY & OFF-PAGE (BACKLINKS)
*Theme: Proving to Google that you are a legitimate, trusted UK business.*

- **Week 9: The Citation Foundation**
  - Spend one entire day manually registering your business details to the top 15 UK directories (Yell, Scoot, FreeIndex, etc.).
  - Set up Connectively / HARO.
  - *Time required: 8 hours.*
- **Week 10: Outreach Commences**
  - Answer 3 Journalist queries via HARO.
  - Find 5 UK Home/Tech blogs. Send Guest Post Pitch email (Email Template #1).
  - Publish Blog Post #5.
  - *Time required: 6 hours.*
- **Week 11: Competitor Stealing**
  - Run Ahrefs Free Backlink Checker on your Top 3 competitors.
  - Email 10 sites that link to them using the Resource Page or Broken Link templates.
  - *Time required: 5 hours.*
- **Week 12: The Quarterly Review**
  - Fill out the Weekly Report Template (File 6C).
  - Analyse the 90 days. Are impressions up? Are clicks moving? Which keywords broke into the Top 50?
  - Plan Quarter 2 based exclusively on the keywords Google is already starting to reward you for.
  - *Time required: 3 hours.*
