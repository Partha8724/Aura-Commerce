# 8. 90-DAY SEO IMPLEMENTATION ROADMAP

## Month 1: The Technical & Structural Foundation
*Focus: Get the site crawlable, indexed, and structurally sound.*

- **Week 1:**
  - Implement `robots.ts` and `sitemap.ts` in your Next.js app router.
  - Implement the `ProductSchema.tsx` component into your Product Page UI.
  - Verify domain in Google Search Console and submit the sitemap.
- **Week 2:**
  - Audit Page Speed (Lighthouse). Fix any large images.
  - Setup Google Analytics 4 (GA4) and link it to Search Console.
  - Set up canonical tags across all pages.
- **Week 3:**
  - Execute the "Manual Keyword Research" framework. 
  - Build out the mapping spreadsheet for your TOP 20 high-margin products and 5 main categories.
  - Apply the AI SEO Agent to rewrite the titles and descriptions for those top 20 products.
- **Week 4:**
  - Write and publish the core Trust pages: About Us, Shipping Policy, Returns Policy, Contact Us.
  - Ensure local UK signals (GBP, en-GB, UK styling) are active site-wide.

## Month 2: Content Velocity
*Focus: Feeding Google unique text data to distinguish your store from other dropshippers.*

- **Week 5:**
  - Apply the Category Page SEO Template to all 5 main categories (add 300 words of optimized text to the bottom of the grid).
- **Week 6:**
  - Create the `/blog` route. 
  - Publish Blog Post #1 targeting an informational long-tail keyword (e.g., "Minimalist Home Decor Trends 2024").
  - Internally link this blog to 3 product pages.
- **Week 7:**
  - Run a batch update using your backend Gemini script to rewrite the descriptions for the next 100 products.
  - Check Google Search Console for "Discovered - not indexed" errors.
- **Week 8:**
  - Publish Blog Post #2.
  - Review Search Console. If specific products are getting high impressions but low clicks, tweak their Title Tags.

## Month 3: Off-Page Authority Building
*Focus: Gaining trust signals via backlinks.*

- **Week 9:**
  - Submit the domain to the 15 UK business directories (Yell, Scoot, FreeIndex).
  - Setup Google Alerts for your brand name.
- **Week 10:**
  - Publish Blog Post #3 & #4.
  - Sign up for Connectively/HARO and spend 15 mins daily answering relevant UK e-commerce/niche journalist requests.
- **Week 11:**
  - Audit competitor backlinks using Ahrefs Free Webmaster Tools.
  - Send 10 Guest Post Pitches to niche UK blogs.
- **Week 12:**
  - Comprehensive Review: Check GA4 organic traffic growth, GSC indexation rate, and average keyword positions.
  - Refine your Keyword Strategy for Month 4 based on what Google has decided to rank you for so far.
