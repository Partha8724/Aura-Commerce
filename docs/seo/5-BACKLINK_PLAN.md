# 5. BACKLINK ACQUISITION PLAN

## 15 Types of Backlinks (Realistic for Dropshippers)
1. **Supplier/Manufacturer Links:** (Harder for AliExpress, but some CJ suppliers or brand-name items might link if you ask).
2. **Niche Directories:** E.g., UK homeware directories, UK fashion directories.
3. **Local Citations:** Register your business address on Yelp UK, Yell.com, Thomson Local.
4. **HARO / Connectively:** Answer journalist queries about "ecommerce trends" or "home decor tips".
5. **Guest Posting:** Write articles for home decor or tech blogs in exchange for a link.
6. **Ego Bait:** Write a "Top 20 UK Interior Designers on Instagram" post and tag them on social media. They might link to it.
7. **Broken Link Building:** Find dead links on decor blogs and suggest your product page instead.
8. **Pinterest Pins:** Creating pins directly links back to your site.
9. **YouTube Descriptions:** If you do video reviews of your dropshipped products, link back in the description.
10. **Reddit / Quora:** Answer questions helpfully. ONLY drop a link if it directly solves the user's specific problem.
11. **Coupon/Promo Sites:** VoucherCodes, HotUKDeals (Requires good reputation).
12. **Unlinked Brand Mentions:** Set up Google Alerts for "Luxe Doow". If someone mentions you without a link, email them.
13. **Roundup Posts:** Search "Best home decor items for spring intitle:roundup". Email the author to be included next time.
14. **Resource Pages:** Search "home decor resources inurl:links".
15. **Podcast Interviews:** Offer to easily talk about "starting an ecommerce dropshipping biz in the UK" on podcasts. They always put your link in show notes.

## Outreach Email Templates

**Guest Post Pitch:**
> Subject: Content idea for [Website Name] - from an e-commerce owner
>
> Hi [Name],
> I've been reading [Website Name] for a while, really loved your recent piece on [Topic]. 
> As I run an e-commerce store specialising in [Niche], I have some unique insights into what UK buyers are currently raving about. I'd love to write a guest post for you titled "5 [Niche] Trends Taking Over British Homes in 2024". 
> I'll ensure it's highly engaging and completely original. Let me know if you're open to this!
> Best,
> [Your Name]

**Broken Link Replacement:**
> Subject: Broken link on your [Topic] post
>
> Hi [Name],
> Just a quick heads up - I was reading your excellent post on [Topic] and noticed one of the links to a [Competitor/Old Product] is returning a 404 error.
> We actually stock a very similar, updated item here: [Your Link]. Might make a good replacement for your readers so they don't hit a dead end!
> Thanks for the great content,
> [Your Name]

## Monitoring Competitors (Free)
1. Identify a competitor indexing well on Google.
2. Sign up for a free Ahrefs Webmaster Tools account OR use Ubersuggest (free searches).
3. Plug in the competitor URL.
4. Look at their "Backlinks" report to see which blogs/sites link to them. 
5. Reach out to those exact same sites pitching your store.
