# 2. ON-PAGE SEO MASTER TEMPLATES

## Product Page SEO Template
*Target: Convert users while satisfying search intent for specific products.*

- **Title Tag (max 60 chars):** [Product Name] - Buy Online UK | Luxe Doow
  - *Good example:* Minimalist Ceramic Vase - Buy Online UK | Luxe Doow
  - *Bad example:* vase home decor cheap dropshipping new
- **Meta Description (150-160 chars):** Buy the [Product Name] in the UK. [1 key benefit]. Free UK shipping available. Curated luxury from Luxe Doow. Shop the collection today.
  - *Good example:* Buy the Minimalist Ceramic Vase in the UK. Perfect matte finish for modern living rooms. Free UK shipping available. Curated luxury from Luxe Doow.
  - *Bad example:* Buy vase. cheap vase. Good quality. 
- **URL Slug:** `lowercase-hyphenated-product-name`
- **H1 Header:** The exact Product Name (must only be one H1 on the page).
- **H2 Headers:** 
  - `Product Details & Specifications`
  - `Why Choose the [Product]?`
  - `Delivery to the UK`
- **Image Alt Text Rules:** `[Product Name] in [Colour / Context] - Luxe Doow UK`
- **Internal Linking:** Link to 3 "Similar Products" in the same category.

## Category Page SEO Template
*Target: Rank for broad head terms (e.g., "Minimalist Home Decor UK")*

- **Title Tag (max 60 chars):** [Category Name] Shop | Luxury [Category] UK | Luxe Doow
  - *Good example:* Home Decor Shop | Luxury Home Accessories UK | Luxe Doow
- **Meta Description (150-160 chars):** Discover our curated collection of [Category Name] in the UK. Elevate your space with premium, affordable [plural category synonym]. Order today.
  - *Good example:* Discover our curated collection of home decor in the UK. Elevate your space with premium, affordable interior accessories. Order today.
- **H1 Header:** [Category Name]
- **Category Description (at bottom of page):** Minimum 300 words. Explain what the category contains, who it is for, and use secondary LSI keywords naturally.
- **Internal Linking:** Link back to the Homepage, and forward to sub-categories if applicable.

## Blog Post SEO Template
*Target: Capture informational search intent ("Top of funnel")*

- **Title Tag (max 60 chars):** [Numbered List / How To] [Topic] | Luxe Doow
  - *Good example:* Top 10 Minimalist Home Decor Trends for 2024 | Luxe Doow
- **Meta Description (150-160 chars):** Looking for [Topic]? Read our comprehensive guide on [Topic details]. Find expert styling tips for your UK home at Luxe Doow.
  - *Good example:* Looking for modern interior inspiration? Read our comprehensive guide on the top minimalist home decor trends. Find expert styling tips for your UK home.
- **H1 Header:** Catchy, emotional headline containing the target keyword.
- **H2 / H3 Structure:** Use H2s for main list items or chapters. Use H3s for sub-points.
- **Internal Linking:** EVERY blog post must link to at least 3 relevant Product pages using exact-match anchor text (e.g., "our favourite *ceramic ribbed vase* fits this aesthetic...").

## Homepage SEO Structure

1. **Top Nav (Header):** Clean semantic `<nav>`. Links to main categories.
2. **Hero Section (H1):** Curated Luxury Dropshipping UK | Luxe Doow (Keep it brand-focused but keyword-rich).
3. **Featured Categories (H2):** Grid of 4 main categories.
4. **Trending Products (H2):** Grid of top-selling items. Link directly to product pages.
5. **Trust Signals (H2/H3):** "Secure Checkout", "UK Customer Support", "Curated Global Suppliers".
6. **Footer:** Links to Shipping Policy, Returns, Contact Us, About Us. (Ensure these exist so Google trusts the site).
