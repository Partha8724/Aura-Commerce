# 3. KEYWORD STRATEGY FRAMEWORK

## Manual Keyword Research via Google.co.uk (No Paid Tools needed)
1. Open an Incognito window and go to Google.co.uk.
2. Type your base product (e.g., "ceramic vase").
3. DO NOT hit enter. Look at the dropdown autocomplete suggestions.
4. Add modifiers like "UK", "buy", "for living room", or colours to see long-tail variations.
5. Search the term. Scroll to the "People also ask" section. These are your blog post titles and FAQ schema questions.
6. Scroll to the bottom "Related searches". These are your category tags.

## Keyword Clustering Method
- **"Buy Now" Keywords (Transactional):** Keywords containing "buy", "price", "shop", "UK delivery", or highly specific product names (e.g., "15w qi wireless charging lamp"). *Map these to Product Pages.*
- **"Research" Keywords (Informational):** Keywords containing "how to", "best", "ideas", "vs", "review". *Map these to Blog Posts.*
