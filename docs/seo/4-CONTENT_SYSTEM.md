## Product Description Formula that Ranks

**Formula:**
1. **The Hook (2 sentences):** Address the customer's pain point and introduce the product.
2. **The Feature/Benefit Bullets (4-5 points):** Short, scannable list. Format: *Feature - Benefit*.
3. **The UK Delivery Reassurance (1 sentence):** Mention shipping times clearly to build trust.
4. **Technical Specs (List):** Dimensions, materials, sizing (crucial for schema and long-tail searches).

## Blog Content Brief Template

**Before giving it to a writer (or AI):**
- **Target Keyword:** [e.g., "how to organise kitchen cupboards UK"]
- **Secondary Keywords:** [pantry storage, space saving, airtight containers]
- **Target Word Count:** 800 - 1000 words.
- **Tone of Voice:** Helpful, premium, British English.
- **Products to Pitch (Internal Links):** Link to the 5-Piece Transparent Food Storage Set, linking on the exact phrase "airtight storage containers".
- **Structure:**
  - H1: 7 Ways to Organise Your Kitchen Cupboards
  - H2: Why Clutter Ruins Your Kitchen
  - H2: Step 1: Decluttering
  - H2: Step 2: Utilising Vertical Space (Link to product here)

## Internal Linking Rulebook
- **Anchor Text Ratio:** 
  - 50% "Brand/Generic" (e.g., "click here", "at Luxe Doow")
  - 30% "Partial Match" (e.g., "check out these *modern vases*")
  - 20% "Exact Match" (e.g., "buy our *white ribbed ceramic vase*")
- **Links Per Page:** A standard blog post should link out to 2-4 internal product/category pages. Do not overstuff.
- **Silo Structure:** Ensure your 'Home Decor' blog posts ONLY link to 'Home Decor' products. Keep topical relevance tight.
